## Features

- **Interactive Flight Search Form**: Supports Round Trip, One Way, and Multi-City routes with origin/destination airport autocomplete, swap control, and passenger counter popup (Adults, Children, Infants, Cabin Class).
- **Search Results & Filters**: Multi-criteria filtering by stops (Non-stop, 1 Stop, 2+ Stops), dynamic budget sliders, time-of-day slots, along with itinerary details, baggage allowance modals, instant WhatsApp lead generation, and email sharing.
- **WhatsApp Lead Generation & Checkout Flow**: Comprehensive passenger checkout flow with client-side validation, generating prefilled, URL-encoded WhatsApp enquiries directed to `+61 452 571 822` with popup fallback modals and reference tracking (`FN-XXXXXX`).
- **Support & Callback Form**: Interactive enquiry form on the Help/FAQ page that converts support queries directly into structured WhatsApp leads.
- **Content & Guides**: Domestic & International deals tables, Top Picks, verified reviews, airline partners showcase, and destination guides for India, Nepal, Pakistan, Bangladesh, and Sri Lanka.
- **Informational & Legal Pages**: About Us, FAQ & Help Center, Australian Privacy Principles compliant Privacy Policy, and South Australia compliant Terms & Conditions.
